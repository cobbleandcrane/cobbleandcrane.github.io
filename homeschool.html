Homeschool rules by state, in plain English

What to file, by when, and what to keep, for each state, with a link to the official source for every rule. Pick your state.

StateFirst thing to do
PennsylvaniaBefore you start (first year): File the affidavit or unsworn declaration with your school district's superintendent.
New YorkJuly 1: Send your notice of intent to the superintendent (or within 14 days if you start mid-year).
VirginiaAugust 15: Send the notice of intent to your division superintendent (list of subjects and proof you qualify).
North CarolinaJuly to April, weekdays 7 a.m. to 3 p.m.: File the Notice of Intent online (not accepted in May or June).
FloridaWithin 30 days of starting: Send a signed notice of intent to your county's district school superintendent.

Need a notice of intent letter? Write one free for New York, Virginia or Florida. It runs in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere.

General information, not legal advice. Laws and forms change, so check the official sources linked here before you file. Checked against the official text on November 24, 2026.
