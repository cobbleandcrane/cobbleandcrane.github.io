Gifts for toddlers who love trucks: 20 ideas from $3 to $60

By Cobble & Crane · November 25, 2026

Some kids are simply truck kids. They stop mid-sidewalk for a garbage truck, narrate every excavator they pass, and know the difference between a loader and a bulldozer before they can spell either one. Good news for gift shopping: truck kids are easy to please. Here are twenty ideas from stocking stuffers to the big box under the tree, sorted by budget, with notes on what works at each age.

First, match the gift to the age

  • Age 2: big, sturdy and simple. Chunky trucks they can push and sit near, board books, things that dump, fill and roll. Skip anything with small parts.
  • Age 3 to 4: pretend play takes off. Hard hats, tool belts, a play dough "construction site", simple puzzles and the first activity books with tracing and counting.
  • Age 5: building and making. Magnetic tiles, bigger puzzles, track sets they design themselves, and activity books with mazes and letters.
Check the age labelFor children under 3, avoid small parts, button batteries and small loose magnets. A toy that says "3+" usually means it has parts that fit in a small mouth.

Stocking stuffers under $10

1. A truck sticker book Age 2+

Reusable sticker scenes of a building site keep little hands busy at restaurants and in the car.

2. A small metal dump truck Age 3+

The kind that fits in a coat pocket and survives the sandbox. Two or three in different colors make a "fleet".

3. Chunky crayons and printed truck pages Age 2+

Roll a few pages into a tube with a ribbon. Our free sampler has tracing roads, counting cones and scissor practice you can print tonight.

4. A name page the truck "painted" Age 3+

Type their name into our name tracing maker, watch a road crew truck paint each letter, then print the tracing page. It makes a sweet gift tag or a first page in a new notebook.

5. A play hard hat Age 2+

Every crew needs a supervisor. Add a kid-size safety vest for the full uniform.

6. A kid flashlight Age 3+

For "night shift" under a blanket fort. Choose one with a screw-closed battery door.

Gifts from $10 to $25

7. Truck books, read-aloud and activity Age 2+

A read-aloud story for bedtime plus an activity book for the kitchen table is a combination that gets used every day. Our Road Crew activity books (Patterns and Counting) are 100 big pages of tracing roads, mazes and counting cones for ages 3 to 5.

8. Play dough with construction tools Age 3+

Rollers, scoops and a few small trucks turn a tub of dough into a road project. A cookie sheet makes the cleanup easy.

9. Pull-back trucks Age 2+

Pull back, let go, race. Hallway races are a great before-dinner energy burner.

10. A chunky wooden puzzle Age 2 to 4

Peg puzzles with trucks for two-year-olds, 12 to 24 piece puzzles for threes and fours.

11. A toy tool belt Age 3+

Pair it with the hard hat and they will "fix" every chair in the house.

12. Sandbox or bath diggers Age 2+

A little excavator that scoops water or sand works year round: bathtub in winter, sandbox in summer.

13. A printable activity pack Age 3 to 5

Print it once and use it all December. Our Christmas Bundle has five truck and Christmas packs in one download.

Bigger gifts from $25 to $60

14. A big sit-near dump truck Age 2+

A large, sturdy plastic truck that hauls blocks, stuffed animals and the occasional sibling's shoe. Look for a smooth tilting bed.

15. Magnetic building tiles Age 3+

Garages, ramps and tunnels for their trucks. Buy from a maker that seals the magnets inside, and keep loose magnets away from younger siblings.

16. A road and track set Age 3+

Wooden road pieces they can rearrange every day. Add a few signs and a bridge.

17. A construction play mat Age 2+

A printed road map rug becomes a city for every truck they own.

18. A toy workbench Age 3+

Screws, bolts and a hammer for the kid who wants to build the truck, not just drive it.

Gifts that cost little or nothing

19. A "machine watching" date Age 2+

Pack a snack and watch a road crew or building site from a safe spot on the sidewalk. Point out each machine and what it does. Write it on a card so they can unwrap it.

20. A library truck stack Age 2+

Borrow every truck book the library has, wrap them in newspaper, and return them in three weeks. A new stack every month.

How to make the gift last past December

Most truck toys get played with for years when they come with a little setting: a box of blocks to haul, a shoebox garage, a tape road on the floor. Painter's tape is the best $3 you will spend. Lay out a road through the living room and watch every new truck drive it.

And if a cold day calls for something quiet, a few printed pages at the kitchen table go a long way. Merry Christmas from the Cobble & Crane road crew.
