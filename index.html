How it works

Bought at bedtime, busy by breakfast

1

Pick a pack

Checkout happens on Etsy, so your payment and your files stay safe in your Etsy account.

2

Download the PDF

It arrives right away. Save it once and keep it for the next rainy day, car ride or sick day.

3

Print and play

Letter or A4, color or black and white. Print a page for each child, then print it again.
