How to homeschool in Virginia (2026-27)

Virginia needs two things a year: a notice of intent by August 15, and evidence of progress by August 1 after the year ends. You qualify through one of four options, and a high school diploma is one of them.

Key dates

  • August 15Send the notice of intent to your division superintendent (list of subjects and proof you qualify).
  • Starting mid-year?Notify as soon as practicable, then meet the requirements within 30 days.
  • August 1 after the school yearSend evidence of progress (a test score or an evaluation).

How you qualify (pick one)

  • You hold a high school diploma
  • You are a teacher with the qualifications the Board of Education sets
  • You provide a program of study or curriculum (a correspondence course, distance learning or another format)
  • You provide evidence that you can give your child an adequate education

Step 1: Notice of intent

Notify your division superintendent in August, no later than August 15, every year. Include a description of the curriculum (a list of the subjects for the coming year) and evidence that you meet one of the four options above. Home instruction can cover a child who turns 5 by September 30, up to age 18.

End of year: evidence of progress (by August 1)

  • A composite score in or above the 4th stanine on any nationally normed achievement test (or an equivalent ACT, SAT or PSAT score), or
  • An evaluation showing adequate progress, such as a letter from a person licensed to teach in any state or a person with a master's degree or higher in an academic field, or a report card or transcript from a college, college distance learning program or home education correspondence school

If no evidence is provided, the program may be placed on probation for a year with a remediation plan. This does not apply to children under 6 on September 30.

Another route

Virginia also allows an exemption from school attendance for bona fide religious training or belief (Code of Virginia 22.1-254). That is a separate process from home instruction.
