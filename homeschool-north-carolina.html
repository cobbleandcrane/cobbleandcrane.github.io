How to homeschool in North Carolina (2026-27)

North Carolina is short and online: file one Notice of Intent with the Division of Non-Public Education, keep attendance and immunization records, and give a standardized test every year.

Key dates

  • July to April, weekdays 7 a.m. to 3 p.m.File the Notice of Intent online (not accepted in May or June).
  • 5 business days before you startFile early enough, and don't withdraw from school until DNPE acknowledges it.
  • Every school yearGive each student a nationally standardized achievement test.
  • At least 9 calendar monthsTeach on a regular schedule (holidays and vacations excluded).
Use DNPE's online Notice of IntentPrintable year kit ($7)

Who must file

Families with a child at least 7 and not yet 16. If your child turns 7 during the school year, file at least five business days before the 7th birthday. File one Notice of Intent per home school, not one per child.

Step 1: Notice of Intent (online)

File through the DNPE online system (a desktop or laptop works best). Include proof that the teacher has a high school diploma or its equivalent. Processing takes 3 to 5 business days, and confirmations come by email (check spam).

School name rules: 30 characters or fewer, not the name of a local school or your curriculum, and none of the words DNPE bans (among them "elementary," "high," "public," "the" and "university"). The name goes on the diploma and can't be changed later.

Who can teach

The chief administrator and every instructor must have at least a high school diploma or its equivalent.

What to keep

  • Attendance records for each student, every year
  • Immunization records
  • Standardized test results (keep at least one year and show DNPE if asked; don't mail them in unless asked)

Keeping records until graduation is recommended.

Testing

Each student takes a nationally standardized achievement test every school year covering English grammar, reading, spelling and mathematics. There are no exemptions.

Closing your home school

Close it in the portal or notify DNPE when it stops operating.
